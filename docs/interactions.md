# Beta UI interactions

The opening notice is a compact “Beta version” badge. Pricing explanations remain at the point of use.

Reviewed product photos open a larger, keyboard-accessible dialog with exact bottle metadata, a product source link, and actions to add the fragrance to a collection or wishlist. Buttons use the same existing bottle-confirmation form. No item is saved merely by opening a photo.

Cards have restrained entrance and hover transitions; dialogs animate in; fill indicators reveal their level. Save notifications use a polite live region. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential animation and transitions.

Light and dark themes share the same interactions. The sun/moon preference remains independent of account storage.

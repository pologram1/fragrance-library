# Beta UI interactions

The opening notice is a compact “Beta version” badge. Pricing explanations remain at the point of use.

Reviewed product photos open a larger, keyboard-accessible dialog with exact bottle metadata, a product source link, and actions to add the fragrance to a collection or wishlist. Known bottle variants save immediately when Add to collection or Wishlist is selected. No item is saved merely by opening a photo.

Cards have restrained entrance and hover transitions; dialogs animate in; fill indicators reveal their level. Save notifications use a polite live region. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential animation and transitions.

Light and dark themes share the same interactions. The sun/moon preference remains independent of account storage.

Add fragrance opens a searchable picker first. It preserves the destination: Wishlist opens a wishlist picker; Overview/My collection open an owned-bottle picker. Known concentrations and sizes appear as separate entries with their existing reviewed images. Selecting a row saves that exact variant in one click. Duplicates in the same list are prevented. Manual entry is a secondary footer action. Catalog records missing required variant metadata use the details form rather than guessing a bottle size.

Wishlist cards include Move to collection. Purchase cost and usage can be entered later with Edit. Shared seller prices link automatically by exact identity, concentration, capacity, and standard/tester type. Optional personal seller offers remain separate.

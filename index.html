How your collection is valued

Match the bottle

Prices belong to an exact fragrance, concentration, size, and bottle type. Testers are tracked separately.

Review the sources

Current prices from the shared seller database link to exact bottle variants. The bottle estimate is the arithmetic average of current, in-stock prices from reviewed sellers, counting each seller once. Shipping, tax, and personal coupons are excluded.

Track value and use

Remaining value is the current full-bottle replacement price multiplied by the fraction of liquid left. Market changes compare with your first recorded market price. This is a proportional estimate, not an actual used-bottle resale quote or realized profit. Discontinued status alone does not change the price.
